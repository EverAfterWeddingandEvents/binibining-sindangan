Dear Valued Partner,

Good day! The Local Government of Sindangan, together with the Binibining Sindangan 2026 Committee, cordially invites you to an exclusive Sponsors’ Meeting and Fellowship with our beloved Hon. Glona G. Labadlabad, Municipal Mayor of Sindangan.

This special evening aims to share the vision, goals, and partnership opportunities for this year’s Binibining Sindangan 2026 — a grand celebration of beauty, culture, and empowerment that continues to bring pride and inspiration to our community.

Your presence will truly mean a lot as we work hand-in-hand in making this year’s pageant another remarkable success story.

For confirmation, please let us know your attendance so we can prepare the proper number of seats to be provided for our valued sponsors. We look forward to spending this inspiring evening with you.
