Dear Valued Partner,

Good day! The Local Government of Sindangan, together with the Binibining Sindangan 2026 Committee, cordially invites you to two special evenings of this year’s pageant.

On December 1, join us at the Municipal Grounds for the Presentation and Reveal of the Official Top 20 Candidates, as our ladies receive their sashes and are introduced to the public for the very first time.

On December 15, we invite our valued sponsors and guests to the Sponsors’ Night, an evening of refined style and celebration together with our Lovely 20 candidates.

For confirmation, please let us know your attendance for each evening so we can prepare the proper number of seats. We look forward to celebrating with you.

Warm regards,

Sharon Joy R. Donguines

Head Organizer – Binibining Sindangan 2026
